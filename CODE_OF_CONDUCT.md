# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, trolling, and other conduct that is not appropriate in a professional collaborative environment.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. Reports should be sent privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled respectfully and confidentially where possible.
